Cosmetics · Egypt

Buy One, Sell Two

Same discount, twice the items

Egyptian cosmetics brand with unstable results. New offers, new funnel: EGP 10.8M at 8×.

Cosmetics brand · Egypt · 12 months (Jun 2025 – Jul 2026)

The problem

  • Results were unstable and swung heavily between campaigns

Offers that raise order value

  • Changed “25% off each item” to “buy one, get the second at 50% off”. The same discount, but only if you buy two
  • A discount that grows with the basket: 15% on one item, 20% on two, 30% on three

Creative

  • Worked with the content and design teams on new creative direction
  • Brought in UGC as a main ad format

Funnel and testing

  • Separate campaigns for cold audiences, the middle of the funnel and retargeting

The slowest month

  • February, the quietest season of the year. The client expected a slow month
  • 3 bundles for Ramadan and Valentine's, and an offer that was hard to refuse
  • UGC with creators who talk like the customer, plus a completely new creative direction
  • EGP 839K in sales at a 7.2 ROAS, +48% vs. the month before
