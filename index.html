Fashion · Egypt

Breaking the Plateau

From EGP 1.6M to 4M in monthly sales

A well-known Egyptian fashion brand, stuck at EGP 1.6M a month. I rebuilt the ad setup and scaled it to ~4M without losing efficiency.

Fashion brand · Egypt · 12 months (Jun 2025 – Jul 2026)

The problem

  • Frequency was high, and the account kept reaching the same people

Two accounts, two jobs

  • Account 1, catalog: kept the catalog ads running, but split the catalog into product sets (summer, winter, autumn, going-out, loungewear) and scaled each one on its own
  • Account 2, creative: a second account to test and scale statics and UGC. It matched the catalog account's results

Scaling

  • Expanded with Advantage+ audiences, interest audiences and lookalikes
  • Peak month: EGP 500K in ad spend

On the website

  • Reshot the product images so each one works as an ad on its own, which lifted catalog performance
  • Added a cross-selling app to raise order value

Cosmetics · Egypt

Buy One, Sell Two

Same discount, twice the items

An Egyptian cosmetics brand with unstable results. I rebuilt the offers and the funnel, and turned it into EGP 10.8M in sales at 8× return.

Cosmetics brand · Egypt · 12 months (Jun 2025 – Jul 2026)

The problem

  • Results were unstable and swung heavily between campaigns

Offers that raise order value

  • Changed “25% off each item” to “buy one, get the second at 50% off”. The same discount, but only if you buy two
  • A discount that grows with the basket: 15% on one item, 20% on two, 30% on three

Creative

  • Worked with the content and design teams on new creative direction
  • Brought in UGC as a main ad format

Funnel and testing

  • Separate campaigns for cold audiences, the middle of the funnel and retargeting
  • 727 campaigns tested, winners moved to scaling

E-commerce · Saudi Arabia & GCC

The Million Riyal Month

A Saudi brand from SAR 200–300K to over 1M a month

Sells on Shopify and Salla, with ads on Meta, TikTok and Snapchat.

Shopify and Salla · Oct 19 – Nov 18, 2026

Follow the money

  • Measured each platform on its own: Meta 3.06, TikTok 2.56, Snapchat 2.11
  • Moved the budget to Meta and cut back the weaker platforms

Testing → Scaling

  • Every new creative starts in a testing campaign; winners get their own ad set in scaling
  • Scaled winners across broad, interest and lookalike audiences
  • Brought back an old creative the client already had. It ended up driving 30%+ of sales

Market by market

  • Separate campaigns for Saudi Arabia and the rest of the GCC
  • A dedicated campaign for the strongest Gulf markets, like Qatar and the UAE

Order value

  • Added bundles that raised the average order value

When demand drops

  • Partnership ads with Gulf influencers
  • New angles tested mid-month and around White Friday, when buying slows down
